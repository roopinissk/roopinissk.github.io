Notes from the faculty-trainee lunch at Yale with Daniel Greif on Oct 28, 2026
Topic: Time management and avoiding burnout

- Academia/research/work is a marathon, it never ends so its always good to keep in touch with hobbies and develop new interests all the time in life
- Work as much as it excites you but at the same time listen to yourself on when to take a step back. A weekend comepletely off work is not going slow you down. 
- Find time to completely shut down your screen and be present with your surrounding. Even a couple of hours off in a busy week can give you strength to keep going. 
- Be intentional with yourself and your surrounding. Foster good relationships and friendships. Its important to listen to your surrounding and not just work. 
- Subjective: Family is important. When you have an obligation to family that crosses with work, prioritising based on importance helps in deciding which one to tend to
- Always communicate to your surrounding. 
- Its good to schedule outings (or calls, whaever works) with friends in advance so you dont cancel on the day of.
