Notes from the faculty-trainee lunch at Yale with Stefania Nicoli on November 18, 2026

Topic: Manuscript writing

The below can apply to any writing in general, not necesarily scientific

I. The overarching theme that was addressed was "Why publish?"

1. In broad, publishing is to communicate to the public. People can benefit from discovery
    - it can be knowledge
    - it can be adopted for a treatment or have other medical applications

2. Recognition/Metric
    - It can be a personal goal/validation of work
    - Instituitional metric that evaluates your worth

II. The steps leading to a publication

1. Putting together
    - Good practice to put figures together irrespective of story. Even if you dont have an idea for a paper
    - Its a nice way of seeing how your research takes shape or changes over time.
    - Deciding a title comes mostly last

2. Editing and refining
    - The journal has an editor who does the first pass, who is a human so its better to have your own pre-editorial group of friends and colleague who read the writing first.
    - Feedback can be difficult to take, but its important to take critical feedback. The more people reviewing your writing, the better it is for the story you are willing to tell the world. 

3. When to stop
    - When you feel like you have done your best.
    - Depends on the urgency of the field. 
    - Learning to give up is as important as moving forward.
